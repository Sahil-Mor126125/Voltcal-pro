How to Size an Off-Grid LiFePO4 Battery Bank (Engineering Formula)

Unlike lead-acid or AGM batteries—which suffer severe cycle-life degradation if discharged below 50%—Lithium Iron Phosphate (LiFePO4) batteries safely deliver 80% to 90% Depth of Discharge (DoD) for 4,000+ cycles. However, ignoring inverter conversion losses and cold-weather voltage sag is the most common mistake in DIY off-grid builds.

Required Bank (Ah) = [ (Daily Wh ÷ Inverter Efficiency) × Autonomy Days × Temp Factor ] ÷ (System Voltage × Max DoD)

Why 48V Beats 12V for Whole-Home Systems

Doubling or quadrupling system voltage cuts DC current proportionally (P = V × I). A 3,000W inverter draws 250 Amps at 12V (requiring expensive 4/0 AWG cables), whereas at 48V it draws just 62.5 Amps, allowing thinner copper cables and higher MPPT solar controller capacity.

Cold Weather Charging Warning

Standard LiFePO4 cells can discharge down to -20°C (-4°F) with a 15–25% capacity buffer, but must never be charged below 0°C (32°F) unless equipped with internal self-heating pads and a low-temperature BMS cutoff.

Understanding Peak Sun Hours & MPPT Controller Sizing

Peak Sun Hours (PSH) does not mean total daylight hours—it measures how many equivalent hours solar irradiance averages 1,000 W/m². While Phoenix, Arizona averages ~6.5 PSH annually, London or Manchester averages ~2.6–3.1 PSH (and drops below 1.2 PSH in December).

Array Watts = Daily Wh ÷ (Peak Sun Hours × System Efficiency)  |  MPPT Amps = (Installed Array Watts ÷ Battery Voltage) × 1.25

Why High-Frequency vs. Low-Frequency Inverters Matter for Surges

Induction motors (well pumps, refrigerators, table saws) draw 3× to 5× their running wattage for 0.5 to 3 seconds during startup. Low-frequency toroidal transformer inverters (such as Victron MultiPlus or Schneider) handle 200% surge loads effortlessly, whereas budget high-frequency inverters often trip on overload unless oversized by at least 25–30%.

DC Voltage Drop Formula & Ampacity Rules

In low-voltage DC systems (12V/24V/48V), voltage drop is calculated over the full round-trip loop (positive + negative cable length = 2 × L). Even a 1.5V drop on a 12V battery cable represents a 12.5% energy loss and can prematurely trigger an inverter's low-voltage cutoff alarm.

V_drop = (2 × Length_meters × Current_Amps × 0.0175 Ω·mm²/m) ÷ Cross_Section_mm²

How Tax Credits & Compounding Utility Inflation Drive Solar ROI

In the United States, the 30% Residential Clean Energy Credit (IRC Section 25D) applies to both solar panels and standalone battery storage (≥3 kWh), immediately cutting upfront capital outlay by nearly a third. In the United Kingdom, residential solar and battery installations benefit from 0% VAT alongside Smart Export Guarantee (SEG) payments up to 15p/kWh for exported surplus.
